# Guidance for Supervisors

### Shared Responsibilities and Accountability

Supervisors play a key role in fostering responsible, transparent, and ethical use of GenAI in postgraduate research. However:

1. Students remain fully responsible for their research outputs, including any use of GenAI tools.
2. Supervisors are not expected to act as AI experts or detection specialists.

Supervisors should:

• Encourage open discussion about GenAI use\
• Direct postgraduate students to institutional and external guidance and training (see [Training and Supports](https://app.gitbook.com/o/-Lg9mnhyV3BJhygA9889/s/TWl4oOy7Wij3GEiGVjhn/~/edit/~/changes/16/training-and-support))\
• Highlight ethical, data protection, and integrity considerations\
• Reinforce the importance of transparency, verifiability, accountability and original content

The University will support supervisors through AI literacy training, research integrity training, approved tools, and institutional guidelines for staff and students.

### Agreeing Expectations Around GenAI Use

At the start of a research project (and revisited as needed), supervisors and postgraduate students should discuss and agree:

• Appropriate and inappropriate uses of GenAI for the project\
• Any GDPR, ethics, or IP considerations\
• How GenAI use will be documented and disclosed

This may be recorded in supervisory meeting notes or through an AI use statement.

### AI Detection Tools

GenAI detection tools are currently unreliable and contested. Supervisors should not use detection software to verify AI. The primary safeguard for responsible use is open dialogue, transparency, and student accountability.

### Suggested Discussion Topics

#### At project outset:

• What GenAI tools are you familiar with?\
• Where might AI support (or hinder) your learning and research quality?\
• What uses are acceptable for this project?\
• How will AI use be documented?

#### During supervision:

• How (if at all) have you used AI tools in this stage of your work?\
• Can you explain and justify all key decisions and analysis?\
• How have you verified AI-assisted outputs?

### Skills Development

Supervisors should consider GenAI use in the context of doctoral training:

• Which core research skills must be developed independently?\
• Where might AI appropriately support efficiency or creativity?\
• Could early independent work followed by AI-assisted refinement support learning?
