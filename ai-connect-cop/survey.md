# Survey

This anonymous 5-minute survey explored how SETU researchers use generative AI tools in their work.  Its main purpose was to inform guidelines being developed by SETU's Working Group on Gen AI in Research and inform the work of SETU's Community of Practice on Gen AI in Research, in particular in terms of topics of workshops and training sessions.
