# Purpose

_AI Connect_ is a Community of Practice established by the Working Group on Gen AI in Research (Subgroup of the AI Steering Committee) that brings together researchers, PhD students and academic staff to explore the ethical and innovative use of generative AI in research.  Launched in April 2025, the initiative supports SETU’s commitment to responsible AI adoption by creating a space for dialogue, collaboration and skills development.&#x20;

Through workshops, guest lectures and collaborative sessions, AI Connect promotes AI literacy, encourages critical reflection on the opportunities and risks of AI, and contributes to the development of institutional guidance for researchers.&#x20;
